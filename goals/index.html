Swiss Brakeman Chasing the Podium

Building Speed and Power for Medal-Winning Performances

My Goals

After competing at the Olympic Games 2026 and finishing 6th with my team, a big milestone is checked off. But competing is one thing - the podium is another.

My goal for the next four years is to get even better and chase medals. I know what it took to reach this level, and I am eager to surpass it. I will train harder, recover better and approach the sport from more angles than just physical training alone.

Working towards phenomenal performances on ice will be my focus every day of the next Olympic cycle.
